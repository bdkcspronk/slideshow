# The 10 commandments of Jeff de Hosson, our honorary member: one per line, in order.
# The slideshow shows one per week, in this order, starting over after the last one; it changes on
# Monday at midnight. Lines starting with # are comments. Changes show up without a restart.
If you can't stand the heat, stay out of the university kitchen and do something else.
Develop every respect for each other's talents and for other novel ideas.
Concentrate on new concepts, not details.
Get joyful inspiration from your academic field of interest.
Discover your own talents and limits.
Become critical and protest, keeping in mind pro-testari (Lat.) stands for testify in favor, not against.
Put the pedal to the metal.
Scire tuum nihil est, nisi te scire hoc sciat alter.
Keep your friends in science close, and your enemies closer.
Concentrate on 'out-of-the-box-thinking', bearing in mind: the closer your experiments match your theoretical predictions and vice versa, the further away you are from a Nobel prize.
